Electrical distribution

At Power pulse, we are dedicated to providing reliable
and efficient electricity distribution to homes, businesses,
and industrie. We monitor the grid in real-time to address
issues swiftly, and we strive to provide excellent customer
service through easy billing,

Automation and control

At Power pulse, automation and control are at the heart of
how we ensure the efficient and reliable delivery of electricity.
By using advanced technologies like smart grids, automated sensors,
and real-time monitoring systems, we are able to optimize power distribution, reduce energy loss, and improve response times during outages.

Electric fence and CCTV installation

At Power pulse, we also specialize in providing security solutions through the installation of electric fences and CCTV systems. Our electric fences offer an effective deterrent to unauthorized access, providing an additional layer of security for both residential and commercial properties.

water treatment equipment

At Power plus, we provide high-quality water treatment equipment to ensure
safe, clean, and reliable water for residential, commercial, and industrial use.
Our range of solutions includes advanced filtration systems, reverse osmosis units, water softeners, and disinfection technologies designed to remove contaminants, improve water quality.
